# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of background, identity, experience level, or role.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Focus criticism on ideas, code, documentation, and evidence rather than people.
- Respect privacy and do not publish personal information, credentials, or private workspace material.
- Assume good faith while leaving room to challenge claims with evidence.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, personal attacks, deliberate privacy violations, and sustained disruption of discussion are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns using GitHub's content-reporting tools or a private contact method published by the repository maintainer on their GitHub profile. Do not use public Issues to disclose sensitive personal details. Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other actions proportionate to the situation.
